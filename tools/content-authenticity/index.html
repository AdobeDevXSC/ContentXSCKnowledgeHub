Provenance, not a promise of truth.
Content Credentials record signed claims about origin and edits. They do not prove that the pictured scene is true, or that every claim is accurate.

Viewer: @contentauth/c2pa-web 0.17.0 (MIT / Apache-2.0) + WebAssembly, loaded from jsDelivr. Images are read in your browser and never uploaded. Remote-only manifests need permission, connectivity and CORS. No external trust list is configured; signer trust is not independently established by this demo.
